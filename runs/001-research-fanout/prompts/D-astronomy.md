TOPIC D — LLM agents and multi-agent systems in astronomy and astronomical archives.

Output path: research/briefs/D-astronomy.md

The audience is IPAC (IRSA, NED, NASA Exoplanet Archive, Euclid, Roman, SPHEREx). Find real,
citable examples of agents doing astronomy work, especially multi-agent ones:
- Multi-agent research systems in astrophysics and cosmology: cmbagent (Xu, Bolliet et al.),
  Denario, "The AI Cosmologist" (Moss), Laverick et al. multi-agent cosmological parameter
  analysis, mephisto (Sun et al.), and anything newer from 2025–2026.
- AstroMLab and related work on LLMs for astronomy; pathfinder for literature search; any
  agent that drives astroquery, TAP, or archive APIs; any MCP servers for astronomy archives
  (ADS, MAST, IRSA, SIMBAD, NED).
- Reported outcomes: what did the agents actually accomplish, and what failed. Any papers that
  report reproducibility or correctness problems with agent-generated analysis.
- Any IPAC, STScI, NOIRLab, ESA, or NASA statements or projects on agentic AI for archives.

Also propose, in a short section titled "Demo ideas for IPAC", 3 to 5 small fan-out tasks an
IPAC scientist could run on day one (e.g., parallel header extraction across FITS files, parallel
archive queries, parallel triage of pipeline logs), each with the archive or tool it would use.
