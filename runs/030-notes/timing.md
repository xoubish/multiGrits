# Timing — total spoken words and fit against the content budget

Method: every slide in `slides/deck.md` (55 content slides across entries 1–27, plus two Sources
slides that are not presented) was read and its spoken text counted by hand — body prose and the
`<p class="cite">` citation paragraph, which the notes mark as read aloud in full. Headings, code
blocks, image/diagram tags, and HTML-comment notes are not counted.

- **Total spoken words: 3,140.**
- **Implied time at 140 words per minute: 3,140 ÷ 140 = 22.43 min ≈ 22:26.**
- **Content budget in `slides/outline.md`: 1,620 s = 27:00** (plus 180 s / 3:00 of Q&A, for a
  30:00 total segment).

**Fit: yes.** 22:26 of implied speaking time against a 27:00 content budget leaves about 4:34 of
slack. Per segment (budget vs. implied):

| B. Architectures | 6:30 | 4:38 | under by 1:52 |

| D. Where it helps and where it does not | 3:30 | 3:21 | under by 0:09 |

| **Total** | **27:00** | **22:26** | **under by 4:34** |

deck's overall slack, and not itself one of the three cut candidates (those are chosen by what is
least essential to the argument, not by which segment is tightest; see `slides/speaker-script.md`
for the full per-slide breakdown and the three cuts, worth roughly 45–75 s of schedule time each if
the talk runs long for reasons outside the slides).
